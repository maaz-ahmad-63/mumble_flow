# Code of Conduct

## Pledge

People who take part in Mumble AI should be able to do that without harassment. This applies to issues, pull requests, and any other project space.

## Expected

- Assume good intent, and be specific when something is wrong.
- Comment on the change, not the person.
- Accept that a maintainer may say no.

## Not accepted

- Insults, slurs, or sexual language aimed at someone.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Repeated contact after someone has asked you to stop.

## Reporting

Open a private report the same way as in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact [ImaduddeenKhan](https://github.com/ImaduddeenKhan). Reports are reviewed by the maintainer. A person who keeps breaking these rules may be blocked from the repository.

This is a short project policy adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
